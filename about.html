ABOUT ME.

I'm Krittika, a builder and creator who thrives where code, design, and imagination meet. I start by digging into the problem, the people, and the details that matter most. Whether I'm designing a web app, prototyping a UX flow, or crafting a visual identity, I aim to make experiences that feel human, intuitive, and unexpectedly unique.My vision is to create work that matters: interfaces that people actually enjoy using, products that feel alive, and designs that stick in memory. I care about clarity, ethics, and impact, whether I'm coding an AI feature or shaping the look and feel of a project. I want my work to spark connection, curiosity, and joy.
